# Geocaching and You

My dad has been geocaching for years. If you're not familiar, I'm confident a
quick web search (I like DuckDuckGo with AI turned off) will bring you up to
speed. I used to geocache with my dad off and on. It was never something
amazingly exciting, but it was something to do, and, looking back, it was good
to do something together.

Over the past 3 years, I've found myself geocaching more. It's still not
exciting, but it gives some purpose to a walk or hike. Something to get out of
the house and away from the endless scrolling. A good way to enjoy the fresh
air. It's something to do with some friends that doesn't involve spending money
or drinking. It also teaches you a lot about your city. I think geocaching and
cycling are the two best ways to explore a new spot. In this day and age, where
everyone is chronically online, I suggest you do just that.
