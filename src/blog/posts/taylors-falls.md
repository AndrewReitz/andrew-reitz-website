It’s November, there’s no snow, no bugs, the sun is out and it’s 60 degrees. So, there is still time to get outside and climb!




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*2CaDz39HtRrwGQDO.jpg)



I headed out with a bunch of not-well rested friends since we had a Friendsgiving feast the night before.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*u-6nFx9pxuoZLYTe.jpg)



I think it total we had 8 people and one dog with us. We went to the Wisconsin side of Interstate Park. Other than having a blast and trying some new problems there isn’t much to write about. Get out and climb before the snow hits!
