# New Blog

I've been slowly quitting social media. I don't think it's been good for me
mentally, or really for society in general. Without social media, it can be easy
to feel like you're losing connection with people, but was that connection real
in the first place? Instead of posting pictures on Instagram, I send them to my
friends and family, but part of me longs for the good old days of Myspace or
early Instagram, where I can see what my friends and family are up to, where I
see something that feels real, where I see photos of friends without ads
everywhere, in chronological order.

So, I created this website and added a blog back in, to let the people I care
about follow along in order. No AI, no ads, no themes. I'm just going to post
things that I feel like posting.

I've pulled in all my older blog posts. I'm honestly scared to look at them; I
assume they're awful, but if they help you out in some way, awesome!

Before I sign off, I started using Pixelfed to post photos. It's essentially an
Instagram clone, except content is in chronological order and fewer brands are
shoved in your face.
