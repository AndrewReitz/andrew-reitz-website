They day after Christmas I set off with a group of friends in a Subaru Outback to Red Rock Canyon, Nevada. Just 15 minutes out of Vegas! I had never been to Vegas, or to Red Rock and was very excited. There were three of us in the car and three crashpads, along with all our camping and climbing gear needed for a week. We would be bringing on more person home with us making this all a very tight squeeze. We would be meeting up with 20 or so people out at the Canyon campground.

We were on the road from December 26th and roll in the night of the 27th. Stay the week and head home January 3rd, getting home sometime on the 4th. The drive was long, but once you get to Colorado the scenery gets more interesting. We stopped in Denver after a 12ish hour drive, and spent the night at a friends place. We wanted to get on the road, and try to make it before sundown to Red Rock so we planned to get up around 4:30am. We did but it was slow moving after a night of enjoying beers and other Colorado offerings.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*mP1AJovFE9cotwIL.jpg)



_(About half of our crew out at the Kraft Boulders)_

Nothing too interesting happens in the car the rest of the way out to Red Rock. We listen to podcasts, sleep and stop at gas stations. The typical long road trip type thing. We make it to the Red Rock Campground around 9pm. A few of our friends grabbed a camp site already and we just find them. Lucky they got a campsite when they did. This place was packed! We setup our tents in the dark, have a fire for an hour or so and hit the hay, excited to go climbing the next day.

The rest of the week goes smoothly. My basic routine is wake up around 6am (8am back in Minnesota), make coffee, make breakfast, hang out and drink more coffee, get read for the day, go bouldering (around 9–10am), boulder till sun down (~4pm), make dinner, hang out around the fire, go to bed, repeat.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*SoVXiFDDh6_4WZJ-.jpg)



_(Some easy climbing on poker chip)_

The bouldering at Red Rock is amazing. There are so many boulders that are very accessible, and so many awesome people hanging having a great time climbing them! It was a blast, although 5 days of bouldering takes its toll on the hands. For the most part us boulders stayed in the Kraft Boulders area, with the occasional adventure into Gateway Canyon. There were about 3 of us that bouldered the whole trip while the rest of our giant group of 20 or so switched up styles, from multi pitch to sport routes throughout the week. Some stayed for a week some for two, and a small few are still there as I write this up.

For new years eve we decided that getting 20 smelly climbers, that haven’t showered in a few days should make it down to the Vegas Strip. We all figured out different hotels and rides, and choice to meet up at The Flamingo Casino for the fireworks. After showering, changing (7 of us in one room), and a little pre-gaming we made our way to the Vegas Strip. Turns out on new year’s eve they have the national guard out. It was my first time to the strip and I honestly think I would have preferred to stay in the desert. The shower was great, but being around this many drunk people was not my idea of fun, especially when I couldn’t myself find a drink. I’ve since been told by others that the strip can be fun, I question their judgment, but I will give it another chance some other time. Fireworks were at midnight being shot off at Caesars Palace. I’ve seen fireworks, so this wasn’t something that was that spectacular.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*eNM5vxWdoIUZsPrD.jpg)



_(On the vegas strip)_

Our way back to the hotel was the most interesting part. The streets were packed like sardines. It was crazy, we even saw a person get punched out in the street. I even saw a few people with babies, in strollers, out in this mess. I don’t know what they were thinking! I would have picked my baby up and left the stroller just to get out of there, but seriously, what made you think having a baby out in Vegas on NYE was a good idea!? I digress… The thing that made the trip into Vegas all worth it was the hot tub at around 2am. It was glorious, maybe I do like the city that never sleeps if they will keep hotel hot tubs open all night.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*2FEjLpY6zihHPh4M.jpg)



The next day was slow to get going for most. Although, my roommate Paul and I were up around 6am, going out to get breakfast. We figured we had plenty of time before anyone else woke up. By the time we got out of the hotel and to the boulders it was between noon and 1. Leaving with only 3–4 hours of sunlight. That’s ok with me though. My tendons were not happy with me.




![image](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*VMOlJ7sKGNVZAAX3.jpg)



_(Going to get breakfast new years day! This was the photo we sent when someone finally asked where we were)_

The last day we convinced the rest of the group to come out to the boulders, so we had a big ol’ party. Some of the comments made by the rope climbers made me laugh. They seemed pretty jealous of us hanging out in the sun enjoying beers and climbing whenever we felt rested.

The last night my crew was in town for we had a birthday party at the campsite, playing different campfire games and having a few drinks. Most of the group was rolling out the next day, but about 10 or so were hanging back and dispersing sporadically throughout the next week.

The car ride home was long, and we decided to do it in one go instead of stopping in Denver. I’m a bad car sleeper so I think maybe in total I got 4 hours of sleep. I finally roll into my house around 2pm Sunday pretty dazed. I unpack as quickly as I can and lounge around. I would go to sleep but I need to stay up and pick up my dog when my friend get’s off work.

Why didn’t I bring my dog? Well I’d never been to red rock before so I wasn’t sure what to expect, and also though there would be a few days were someone would drag my butt up a roped route, and wouldn’t know what to do with the dog, but somehow I managed to avoid that.

I would totally recommend going to Red Rock if you get the chance. I for one know I would love to go back.
